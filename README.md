# Almanac & Pagan Calendar

Moon phases, sunrise and sunset, festivals, and a regional weather forecast, in one app.

## Use it

Open the app's web address in a browser, then:

- **Phone:** choose **Add to Home Screen** (iPhone) or **Install app** (Android). It then opens full screen.
- **Computer:** choose **Install app** in Chrome or Edge.

## Offline

After your first visit, the app opens without a connection. Moon phases, sunrise and sunset,
and festivals are calculated on your device. Weather shows the last forecast you loaded.

## Set your region

Enter a name, latitude and longitude, and time zone under **Region**, then choose a festival tradition.
Weather updates when you're online.

## Sync between devices (optional)

To keep your region and tradition the same on several devices, use **Sync across devices**.
It stores your settings in a private GitHub Gist, using a GitHub token that you create yourself
with only the Gists permission. Your token stays on your device.
